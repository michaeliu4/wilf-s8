# Bibliography and source audit — revision 8

Updated on 23 September 2026. All **25** bibliography entries are covered
below. Bibliographic identity and mathematical applicability are different
checks: a matching DOI is not proof that a theorem applies. This record
states which exact sources/passages were read and which original proofs
were not reread. It is not a guarantee of priority or a claim that every
reference was read cover to cover. No third-party article is redistributed.

The bibliography follows published identities where available. Preprint
numbering is identified at the citation where it matters. Source-derived
facts are distinguished from the present paper’s reformulations and proofs.

## Ray–West passage-level map and scope of the comparison

Printed pages (not PDF viewer indices): p.57 gives the conjectural degree
clause and unpublished Bloomberg attribution; p.64 defines active sites;
p.67 defines triples; p.68 contains Proposition 5.3. On pp.80–83,
inequalities (16), the contiguous track support, Proposition 8.5,
Corollary 8.6 and Theorem 8.7 support the imported type-C statement.
The correction counts active sources, not source–target pairs.

In the unreflected orientation, eta_2=21 is forced for the active source;
the inserted order is unrestricted in the general triple definition.
Horizontal reflection gives source order 12. The first form of their
track catalogue is reparametrized here by

```
h1=a-2, h2=2(s+1), h3=l-a-2s
w1=b-2, w2=2(t+1), w3=l-b-2t
h=s+1, w=t+1
```

Their compatibility condition becomes l=a+b+t+s-2. Their second forms
have an increasing target and correspond to nonprimitive supports ending
in their maximum. The manuscript supplies the primitive-support selection,
count and interval statistic, not a newly discovered track family.

Corollary 9.5 (p.86) gives the first two fixed-codimension coefficients.
Proposition 9.6 (p.87) already proves monotone polynomiality using tableaux.
Page 88 contains the 1234/1324 example, with attribution to earlier work.
Rendered original pages were used for the block forms, inequalities,
conjecture and these displayed statements.

The manuscript’s new cubic comparison is an internal proof, not a claim
found in Ray–West. It disproves the degree-bound clause when read as
pattern-independent coefficients in degrees at least k. It neither
contradicts their leading-term theorem nor disproves polynomiality of
all possible specified pattern families.
